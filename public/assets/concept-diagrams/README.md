# Concept diagrams

30 original, static SVG illustrations for the existing 30 lessons. Generated by `scripts/generate-concept-diagrams.mjs`; text, scope and references live in `lib/concept-explanations.mjs`.

These are teaching schematics, not manufacturing drawings. Physical cross-sections, circuit relations, process flows and business-role maps are identified separately. Counts, sizes, geometry and routes are simplified and qualified in each visible caption. No company logo, external graphic or AI image is reproduced in these files.

Examples: planar nMOS with separated gate/oxide/channel, DRAM 1T1C electrical relation, NAND page/block hierarchy, ALD's separated dose/purge steps, oxidation consuming silicon, TSV with insulation and joining, and HBM with die stack/interface/interposer/package distinctions.

New primary references include MIT, UC Berkeley, ROHM, Oxford Instruments and Intel. Existing lesson references remain in place. The historical Berkeley lecture is used for fundamental circuit operation, not current product specifications. A Micron PDF surfaced in search but returned 404 on opening and was excluded from the shipped references.

SVG files contain only vector geometry and local text. No scripts, event handlers, remote assets, embedded images or fonts.
